Install in 2 minutes.

The beta isn't signed by Apple yet. macOS will ask you to confirm the first time: that's expected.

  1. Download and drag

    Open Halto.dmg and drag Halto to the Applications folder.

  2. Allow it to open

    On first launch, confirm in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

  3. Turn on Accessibility

    Halto needs it to know which file is under your pointer.

  4. Hold ⌥

    Hover over a file in the Finder and hold ⌥: the wheel opens.

“Apple could not verify Halto”? Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about Halto and click Open Anyway. You only need to do this once.

FAQ

Why does macOS show a warning?

The beta isn't signed with an Apple Developer certificate yet. The app hides nothing and everything runs locally. The public release will be signed.

Do my files leave my Mac?

No. Halto only uses tools built into macOS. No files and no statistics are sent.

Can Halto damage my files?

No. Every action creates a new file next to the original and never overwrites it.

How do I update?

Come back to this page and download the latest version: it replaces the old one and keeps your settings.

How do I uninstall?

Quit Halto from the menu bar, then move the app to the Trash.
